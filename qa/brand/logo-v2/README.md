# Small Talk logo v2: 3 concept directions (st-fable, 13 Oct 2026). Concept only; nothing installed

**The owner's ask:** a designer's refinement of the current logo, more memorable, with a hint of the nail-painting
moment built into the "l" or "k" at a word's end. Polished; not an icon.

**Method:** precise vector edits on the official traced logo (`artwork/hd/logo/small-talk-logo.svg`, #001df9). Each
direction removes only the edited part of one letter and redraws it in the same stroke logic. It's one colour with
knockouts (no gradients), so it reverses cleanly. Everything else is the official logo, untouched. No illustration was
needed.

**Files:**
- `LOGO-V2-SHEET.png`: the owner sheet. Each row shows:
  - the hero;
  - the colourways: blue on cream / cream on brand blue / black on white;
  - the real site header at 1536 and 390 (the build preview, with the logo swapped in the browser only);
  - the favicon at 32 / 16 px, real size and 4×, for the full mark and for the monogram.
- `LOGO-V2-DETAIL.png`: large crops of the edited letters.
- `logo-v2-{A,B,C}.svg`: the concept vectors (layered masks; final exports get flattened after approval).
- Scripts: `make_logo.py` (the geometry), `sheet.py`.

| Direction | The edit | Why it works | Watch-out |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A: nail "l"** (recommended) | The final "l" of "small": its ascender becomes an almond press-on nail (convex sides, a soft point leaning right, a slim gloss crescent). Two small side notches mark where nail meets "finger". The first "l" keeps its wedge, so the pair reads as plain + painted. | The most memorable: the "moment" sits right at the end of "small", it reads as an "l" first, and the silhouette survives at header size | At a glance the tip can read as a lipstick or bullet top. The gloss and almond point carry "nail"; the owner should judge it at real size |
| **B: brush "k"** | The "k" leg becomes one polish-brush stroke: full weight at the join, swelling, then drawing out to a fine flick with two bristle streaks. | The literal painting gesture, and quietly elegant on large uses (packaging, social) | Nearly invisible in the 56 px header and the favicon; the k loses its foot serif |
| **C: bead "k"** | The "k" arm ends in a glossy teardrop terminal (a bead of polish) instead of its flag serif. The logo's "a" already has a teardrop terminal, so it belongs to the type system. | The most typographic and timeless; reads as type first. The drop stays visible as a dot at small sizes | The subtlest: it may feel like a type detail more than a nail story |

**Recommendation: A.** In one line: it's the only one that makes the logo itself say "nails" at every size, while
staying a confident version of the same stacked serif wordmark.

**Favicon / monogram:**
- The full stacked mark at 32 / 16 loses every detail; that's the same as today.
- The monograms: A = the nail "l" alone (reads as a nail at 32, a capsule at 16); B and C = the "k" with its detail.
- If A is picked, I'd recommend the monogram for 16 / 32 and the full mark for 180 / 512.

**After approval:** the export set (header SVG flattened to one path, transparent and square PNGs, favicon set,
social share, checkout + email logo, Shopify Files), then the normal build window and the owner's publish.
