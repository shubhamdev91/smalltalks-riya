# Logo round 2: designer exploration (st-fable, 13 Oct 2026). Concept only; nothing installed

Round 1 (letter-tweaks on the current mark) was rejected by the owner. Brief for this round: think like a brand logo
designer; diverse directions; each must be remembered in a second; include the owner's idea (the k of "talk" as a finger
with a small polish brush on it, or the k as a nail) and my own.

Principle used: ONE idea per mark, a clean silhouette, and it must still read as a 16 px favicon. Everything is real
vector built from the brand's OFL fonts (fontTools outlines) plus hand-drawn shapes; one colour (--st-logo-blue) with
knockouts, so every mark reverses cleanly. `explore.py` regenerates the sheet and the SVGs (`r2-N.svg`, `r2-N-icon.svg`).

`EXPLORE-SHEET.png`: for each direction, hero size, header size (48 px tall on cream) and the favicon at 32 / 16 px, real
pixels and 4x.

| # | Direction | The one idea |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | k = a finger, brush on the nail | the owner's idea as type: the k's upright is a finger with its nail; the k's arm is the polish brush painting it; one line |
| 1b | the same k, stacked | in today's stacked layout; the brush lies flat and comes in from the right |
| 2 | k = a finger, no brush | the quiet version: finger + nail only; arm and leg are the font's; the stacked layout unchanged |
| 3 | the set | five nails fanned like fingertips: a hand tip-on, and literally the product (a set) |
| 4 | "quote nails" | quotation marks are nail-shaped already: the name in quotes made of four press-ons; the quotes alone are the icon |
| 5 | a wave in the bubble | a speech bubble with three fanned nails knocked out: fingertips waving "hey" |
| 6 | a small "small" | the name is the joke: tiny "small", big soft wonky "talk"; pure type |
| 7 | "st" on a coffin nail | monogram badge; the weakest (the coffin reads as a thimble at a glance) |
| 8 | the bubble | the name inside one chunky bubble whose tail is a nail; a sticker |

Designer's view: 4 is the strongest single device (talk + nails in one glance, survives 16 px, no explaining); 1 is the
owner's idea done well, but the brush only lives at big sizes (at favicon size it is just a good k); 3 is the best pure
icon; 8 is the most "merch". 2, 5, 6 are solid; 7 can go.

Next: the owner shortlists 1–3; I refine those (weight, spacing, stacked/one-line, colourways, real header renders) before
any export. Nothing is installed until the owner finalises one.
