Baseline: brief Appendix B. I kept its wording except where noted (“tightened”). Fable owns SEO/AEO strategy, so these are word proposals for that session to check against strategy and live routes. Native SEO fields on products and pages are shared data, so every row needs separate review before it is written.
v1.2 applies st-fable’s review `seo/05-seo-review.md` (R1–R17) under `seo/STRATEGY.md`. The R-numbers in the notes point to it. Shop route is `/collections/all`, and its title/meta are theme-local (R1).
Banned in all metadata and alts: “best”, wear time, “reusable”, “non-damaging”, materials, kit counts, delivery geography/speed (including “India” / “in India”), reviews/ratings, “Almond” or any other shape word (G14), and “original illustrated packaging” unless the owner confirms the art is original (R17). Helper pages (Help topics) may state the shipping region because they repeat the policy itself; metadata may not. “the talks” stays lowercase.
Gate IDs and decisions: see `00-register.md`.
