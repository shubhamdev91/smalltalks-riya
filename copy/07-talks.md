# 07 — the talks (real feature: blog `the-talks`)

Copy register v1.3 · 9 Oct 2026 · the copy session writes nothing to Shopify. The orchestrator publishes the shared rows.

Owner decision, 9 Oct: the talks is a real feature.
- Each thread is a Shopify blog article posted by **Small Talk**.
- Replies are native comments under any alias, published straight away.
- Email is required to reply and never shown.
- “Start a talk” suggestions go to the store email; Small Talk may post them as threads.

Facts: `FACTS.md` F14. Specs: `spec/talks.md` (hub = blog index) and `spec/conversation.md` (thread = article template). These rows supersede the old page rows PG-062…103.

Voice: minimal, Reddit-ish, playful, for 18–24 urban desi readers. Gender-neutral. Gossip about situations, never about people. No real names, no real institutions, nothing defamatory. No invented counts, dates or replies: every number and date on the page comes from Shopify. Never write the word “college” or “campus” (locked rule).

Batch **B5** = the talks. ↗ and → are icons with `aria-hidden="true"`.
