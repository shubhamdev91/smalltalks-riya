Re-visualise these four Small Talk boards so they look like they belong to the attached references and the attached packaging. Same pages, same structure, same copy, same product. Better vibe. Work economically: one pass per board, no exploratory variants unless I ask.

**Locked, do not change**
- The seven nail designs, exactly as on the packaging. Never invent or recolour a nail.
- The packaging art and the stacked royal-blue "small talk" logo. Small packaging refinements are allowed; the illustration, name and headline on each box are not.

**Who it's for.** 18–24, urban desi, independent. Each set is a small moment where she naturally shows her hand. The nails punctuate the moment; they don't cause it. No empowerment speeches, no mascots.

**The vibe to pull from the references.** Desi pop-collage: torn paper, Devanagari-Latin mashups, block-print and ajrakh borders, stamps, margin notes, hand-drawn hearts. Vintage Bollywood poster rendering: bold hand lettering, duotone photo treatments, halftone grain, flat saturated colour fields. Henna'd hands doing things (chai glass, can, cards, phone). Retro-illustrated women with winged liner, bindi, jhumkas, a side-eye. Warm paper under everything, never white screen. Convert the energy; do not copy real brand logos (no Coca-Cola, no Armani), no smoking, no profanity, no film stills.

**Colour system for chrome (apply everywhere outside campaign art).** Royal blue #1013B3: logo, buttons, links, selected states. Deep ink #1F1A2E: product names, shop and PDP headings. Plum #4A243D: editorial display headings only (the talks, Journal, Our Story). Ivory #FFFDF5 ground, lilac tint #EEE8F5 callouts, #D8CFDA dividers. Red appears only inside campaign artwork and errors. The current boards use red for every heading; remove that.

**Fix while re-visualising**
1. Bring the collage language in as texture and framing on the shop, PDP, bag, journal, talks and help pages: block-print section edges, torn-paper joins, stamp chips, kraft behind the Journal, handwritten margin notes beside prices and sizes, a faint Devanagari echo of the set name on each campaign chapter. Accent, not wallpaper; prices, buttons and forms stay fully legible.
2. Hero character crops: half-face or eye compositions with feathered silhouettes dissolving into the background colour. No rectangular crop edges, no overlap with headline or finger. Props: a bill, notebook with crescent-bag sketch and blue pen, unbranded can, pink rotary phone, dupatta with safety pin, cards facing her, two connected hands.
3. Next-set hand: about 20–24% of hero width, four visible nails, thumb hidden, never touching the headline.
4. Landing rhythm: one campaign world owns roughly 70% of a phone screen; one-line story beat, nail row, art, CTA, dashed thread that never crosses text, nails or buttons.
5. Three type voices only: bold curved display, thin handwritten (always smaller than the headline), clean readable sans for everything functional. Legible at 16px on a phone.
6. The talks: whitespace-led, "Example conversation" label, no fake counts, no chai icon, private "Start a talk" form, plum heading.
